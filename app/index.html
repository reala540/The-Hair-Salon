The Gallery story

Good people, performing at the top of their game — right here on Main Street.

For more than three decades, Hair Gallery has been Independence's living room for great hair. Tucked into a historic storefront on West Main, we've built our name the old-fashioned way: honest work, warm conversation, and clients who keep coming back — generation after generation.

From a child's first haircut to a complete color transformation, every appointment starts with a real consultation. We listen first, then we cut, color, and style with the care your hair deserves.
